# Apex Retail Ghana Ltd. | Analyst Take-Home Brief

**Assignment:** Retail Sales Performance & Business Operations Analysis  
**Role:** Junior Data Analyst  
**Business period:** 1 January 2025–31 December 2026

## Assignment

Apex Retail Ghana Ltd. needs a reliable view of sales performance and business operations across its Ghanaian stores and digital channels. Management has seen changing sales, uneven product and regional results, differences in customer and salesperson performance, and occasional returns. The available information is split across raw operational exports.

Prepare an auditable analytical deliverable that helps management understand performance, investigate the questions in the README, and decide where further action may be justified. The data is fictional and supplied for this exercise only.

## Your objectives

- Assess the source data and make its limitations visible.
- Prepare a documented, analysis-ready Excel/Power Query workflow.
- Design a suitable relational model and calculate clearly defined KPIs.
- Build an interactive Power BI report that answers the business questions.
- Present concise, evidence-based findings and recommendations.
- Translate your findings into management follow-up actions in an Asana project named **Sales Performance Improvement**.

## Scope and constraints

Use the eight raw CSV exports under `data/raw/`. The transaction export contains order-line records and intentionally has no revenue or profit calculations. Returns are a separate export. Establish and document how statuses, discounts, costs, returns, duplicates and data-quality exceptions affect your definitions. Keep raw files intact and make material assumptions reviewable.

The assignment evaluates your analysis and judgment. Create the workbook, transformations, model, measures, report, insights and follow-up actions yourself. This brief does not provide a completed solution, prescribed charts, formulas, DAX or conclusions.

## Required work products

See the README for the full field dictionary, business questions, tool requirements, deliverables and self-evaluation checklist. Submit a cleaned/structured Excel workbook, documented Power Query preparation, Power BI report, model, DAX measures, short management insights document and the Asana follow-up project.

## Review standard

Your work should be accurate, reproducible and clearly communicated. Reviewers will look for sound data-quality judgment, defensible metric definitions, a model that behaves correctly, visuals matched to questions, and recommendations linked to evidence and limitations. You should be able to explain the reasoning behind your decisions.
